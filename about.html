About

I am a PhD student in Intelligent Systems Engineering (Neuroengineering) at Indiana University, advised by Silvina Ferradal. I make optical brain imaging of infants and animals anatomically accurate and fast: MRI segmentation, FEM head mesh, GPU photon transport, reconstruction.

In the Ferradal Lab I built the MRI-to-mesh-to-Jacobian pipeline for a first-in-large-animal high-density diffuse optical tomography (DOT) study with the University of Pennsylvania and Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, cut a forward-solve step from about 1400 s to 8 s on the GPU (validated against the MATLAB reference), and presented the system as a first-author oral at the Optica Biophotonics Congress 2026.

I am looking for a summer 2027 research visit, mid-May to late August, with a group working on diffuse optics, fNIRS, head modeling or neonatal neuroimaging.
