Amit Subhash, Neuroengineering PhD student, Indiana University

Amit Subhash is a PhD student in Intelligent Systems Engineering (Neuroengineering) at Indiana University, advised by Silvina Ferradal. He makes optical brain imaging of infants and animals anatomically accurate and fast: MRI segmentation, finite-element head meshes, GPU photon transport and diffuse optical tomography (DOT) reconstruction.

He built the MRI-to-mesh-to-Jacobian pipeline for a first-in-large-animal high-density DOT study with the University of Pennsylvania and Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, cut a forward-solve step from about 1400 s to 8 s on the GPU, and presented the system as a first-author oral at the Optica Biophotonics Congress 2026 (OT2D.3).

He contributes to open-source neuroimaging tools, including pyRiemann, NeuroDOT, NIRFAST, CEBRA and openlifu-python. Before the PhD he completed an MS in Data Science at Indiana University.
